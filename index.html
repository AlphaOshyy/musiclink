WHY IT EXISTS

Independent music has enough moving parts.

MusicLink helps independent artists evaluate musical fit first, then move selected collaborations through a clear process. The prototype is a starting point for a full stack HNDSE project.

BUILT FROM TWO WORLDS

Software engineering × music.

MusicLink is a browser prototype for blind auditions, transparent compatibility scoring and independent music workflows. The current demo uses local browser state and does not yet provide real accounts or server-side storage.
